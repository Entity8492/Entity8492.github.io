PIXEL SWARM

Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 November 2026 · applies to the game on Android, PC, Mac, Linux and the web

In short

Pixel Swarm does not collect, store or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Data saved on your device

Your progress, characters, settings and records are saved only on your own device. They are never sent to the developer. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them.

Online multiplayer

Online co-op is optional. When you host or join a room, your device connects directly to the other players' devices. To set up that connection, the game uses the free PeerJS connection service (peerjs.com) and Google's public STUN server, which see your device's IP address while connecting. The other players in your room see your hero's name and appearance and receive game data such as your position, during that session only. Pixel Swarm has no chat, and the developer stores nothing from a session.

Permissions

The game uses internet access (for online multiplayer only) and vibration (for touch and controller feedback). It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

Children

Pixel Swarm does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. For questions, contact the developer through the Pixel Swarm page: zenerationzero.itch.io/pixel-swarm.
